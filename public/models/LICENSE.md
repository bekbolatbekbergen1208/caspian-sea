Daniel Buscombe (2024), *Doodleverse/CoastSeg Segformer models for 4-class (water, whitewater, sediment and other) segmentation of PlanetScope, Sentinel-2 and Landsat-5/7/8/9 visible-band (RGB) images of coasts. Global version.*

The original release v4 TensorFlow weights have been converted to ONNX by CaspianWatch AI. No retraining or class changes were performed. The manifest records source MD5, ONNX SHA-256 and TensorFlow/ONNX parity verification. Exporting formats does not establish accuracy on the Caspian coast.
